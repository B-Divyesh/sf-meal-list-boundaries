How your meal plan data is handled

The planner works without an account or cloud database.

Short version: meal plans stay in your browser. We cannot read them.

Data stored on your device

The app stores places, meals, ingredients, bought status, templates, and your theme choice in your browser.

If you buy Field Kit, the app also stores your license and its latest verification result.

You can export a JSON backup. Your browser settings can erase all saved data.

Shared links and QR codes

A shared link contains only the selected shopping list. The list is encoded in the URL.

The app does not upload that list. Anyone with the URL can read it.

Purchases

Sociobot and Dodo process the details needed for a Field Kit purchase or refund.

The app sends only your license to Sociobot for verification. It sends no meal or ingredient data.

Network and tracking

The app has no ads, analytics, social trackers, retailer calls, or third-party fonts.

Static hosting may keep security logs with an IP address and request time.

Your choices

You can use the free planner without an account or purchase.

Export a backup before clearing browser data if you want to keep your plan.
